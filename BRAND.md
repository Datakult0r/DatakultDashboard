# Brand System — Clinic of AI

## Brand Overview
Clinic of AI builds AI products and services for low- and mid-tier companies, with a near-term focus on five industries: manufacturing, real estate, hospitality, healthcare, and agriculture. The company operates on a diagnostic metaphor — assess what a business needs, identify where it underperforms, prescribe targeted AI solutions. Not a generalist AI lab, not a hype-driven startup. A practitioner — rigorous, methodical, outcome-focused.

Reference brands: Mistral.ai and General Intelligence Company — restrained, editorial, confident, free of visual noise.

## Color System

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| background | #fff8f3 | Primary canvas, warm off-white beige |
| primary-dark | #001215 | Typography, structural lines, grounding (deeper variant) |
| primary-dark-lifted | #002a2e | Slightly lifted dark teal variant |
| accent-deep | #a14000 | Burnt orange — emphasis in professional contexts |
| accent-bright | #ff7a32 | Bright orange — focal accent, diagnostic marker |

Orange is always a precision element, never a fill or wash. Small concentrations: a single dot, a highlighted callout, a thin underline.

### Forbidden Colors
- Neon hues
- Purple-to-pink SaaS gradients
- Glowing electric blues
- Cyberpunk magenta or cyan
- Any "glow-on-dark" treatments

### Dark Mode
Canvas: #001215 or #002a2e. Must feel like ink on paper, not a screen in a server room. No glow effects, no neon edges, no saturated highlights.

## Typography

- Family: Single high-quality grotesque/neo-grotesque sans-serif (Inter, Sohne, GT America)
- No serif, no monospace except technical labels at small sizes
- Hierarchy through size and weight contrast, not color or decoration
- Display headlines: large, regular/medium weight, tight tracking, generous whitespace
- Body: comfortable reading size, neutral tracking, generous line height
- Casing: sentence case for headlines and body
- Technical labels: small caps or numbered prefixes ("01 —", "02 —")

### Forbidden Typography
- Drop shadows, glows, outlines, gradient fills on text
- Condensed/extended tech-bro display faces
- Italics for display
- ALL-CAPS headlines (small caps in technical labels allowed)
- Decorative or novelty typefaces

## Layout

### Hero/Marketing Mode
Full-scale sparse whitespace. Single subject, generous margins, minimal text. Confidence through emptiness.

### Content-Dense Mode
Grid-based density. Left-aligned editorial structure, magazine-like rhythm, modular grid, well-defined zones.

### Rules
- Left-aligned editorial throughout
- Asymmetric balance preferred over symmetrical centering
- Text beside image (split layouts), never overlaid on photographs
- No centered marketing copy without hierarchical anchor

### Signature Hero
Centered object with technical callouts radiating outward — exploded-diagram treatment showing real industry objects with annotation lines to numbered/labeled callouts.

## Voice

- Headlines: direct, declarative, single clause, no exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions
- Captions: clinical, numbered ("01 — Inventory audit. 14-day cycle.")
- Preferred vocabulary: potential, growth, reach, diagnose, optimization, rigorous
- Forbidden vocabulary: AI-powered, revolutionary, disrupt, unleash, transform, next-generation, cutting-edge, harness the power of
- No emojis, no exclamation marks, no casual/conversational filler

## Anti-Patterns (never use)
- Cyberpunk, robot/cyborg imagery, neural networks, holographic UIs
- Floating brains, data tunnels, Matrix code, robot hands
- Neon palettes, SaaS gradients, glow-on-dark
- Tech-bro fonts, gradient text, decorative typefaces
- Floating dashboard mockups, collage density, no-hierarchy centered copy
- Hype language, fake testimonials, aspirational lifestyle imagery
